⚽ Basic rules

Learn the pitch, teams, kick-off, throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, fouls and offside.

Open lesson

A match usually has two teams of 11 players. The aim is to score more goals than the opponent. The referee applies the Laws of the Game. A player is not penalised for offside merely for standing in an offside position; involvement in active play matters.

🧭 Positions

Understand the job of each role.

Open lesson
  • Goalkeeper: protects the goal and starts attacks.
  • Centre-back: defends central spaces.
  • Full-back: defends wide areas and may support attacks.
  • Midfielder: connects defence and attack.
  • Winger: creates chances from wide areas.
  • Striker: leads the attack and tries to score.

🎯 Passing

Improve decision-making and passing basics.

Open lesson

Scan before receiving, open your body, use the right weight of pass and move after passing. A simple pass that keeps possession can be better than a risky pass.

🛡️ Defending

Learn pressure, cover and balance.

Open lesson

The first defender slows the ball carrier. A teammate provides cover, while others protect dangerous spaces. Stay balanced and guide the attacker away from the centre when possible.

🔥 Pressing

Understand team pressure.

Open lesson

Pressing works best when teammates move together. Pressing triggers can include a poor touch, a backward pass or a pass toward the sideline. If the team is stretched, pressing can be bypassed.

🧠 Game intelligence

Scan, position, decide and move.

Open lesson

Check your surroundings before the ball arrives. Offer a passing angle, keep useful distance from teammates and move into space after releasing the ball.
